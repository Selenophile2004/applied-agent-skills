# Structural Completeness and Overlap Audit

## Inputs

Existing categories/options/workplan, scope, decision use and edge cases.

## Deliverables

Overlap/gap diagnosis, revised categories, inclusion/exclusion rules and placement stress test.

## Applied request

~~~text
Audit a customer segmentation and test a customer currently assigned to two categories.
~~~

## Acceptance criteria

No double counting within the chosen dimension; exclusions and irreducible ambiguity are explicit; mixed levels are corrected.
