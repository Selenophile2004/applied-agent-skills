# Visual Art Direction

Use for the visual identity of a site, campaign, dashboard or product surface. Establish how the work should feel and what its composition should communicate. For detailed behavior and reusable component specifications, hand off the selected direction to the relevant interaction or design-system workflow.

## Frame the brief

Identify the audience, purpose, most important message, required content, target surfaces and languages. Separate binding brand rules from preferences and assumptions. Check which assets are available and suitable for use. Define success in observable terms, such as "a first-time visitor can identify the offer and primary action from the first screen."

## Explore and develop

1. For open exploration, create two or three directions with a distinct organizing idea. Keep the core content and primary action comparable. Vary the composition, type hierarchy, image treatment and motion grammar. For each direction give a key-screen sketch or mockup, a narrow-screen adaptation and a meaningful tradeoff. Follow an existing direction for revisions.
2. Connect the design to the content. An editorial direction might give the strongest evidence typographic prominence and annotate it in a quieter layer. A spatial direction might organize the same evidence around relationships. Use references to identify principles and develop a fresh composition with authorized assets.
3. Develop the chosen direction, or the best-fit direction with stated rationale if the user delegates the choice. Define the grid and content order, spacing rhythm, type roles, color roles, focal imagery, crop behavior and motion triggers with a reduced-motion fallback. Specify which elements adapt on narrow screens. Keep essential text as text and account for RTL reading order.
4. When a visual artifact is requested, inspect the result at representative wide and narrow sizes. Check focal point, legibility, contrast, asset crops, text expansion and consistency. Distinguish rendered checks from checks still pending. Use the available host tools for the requested deliverable.

## Deliver

Provide the direction or comparable concepts, rationale, a key-screen mockup or implementation-ready spec, asset needs and material unresolved tradeoffs. For handoff, include one example of how the visual rules extend to a second surface. Keep identity choices specific to the project and portable across authoring tools.
